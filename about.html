About Me

I'm Priyanshu – but most people know me as Pri. I'm a 3D artist and editor who sees the world a little differently: through layers of color, fragments of motion,

fragments of motion, and moments frozen in silence.

My creative journey started out of curiosity and stayed because of obsession. a smoky glitch edit, or a reel stitched from raw feeling, I try to pour a bit of myself into everything I create.

I build things that move, speak, and sometimes just *exist* quietly. Whether it’s a surreal render,

a smoky glitch edit, or a reel stitched from raw feeling, I try to pour a bit of myself into everything I create.

I believe art should feel like a memory you can’t quite place – vivid, emotional, and real. I’m inspired by music, late-night city lights, the smell of rain, black as a feeling, and the soft chaos of life around me.

When I’m not working on visuals, you’ll probably find me dreaming about new concepts, exploring new textures, editing with a midnight playlist on loop, or drinking Coke under a summer sky.

Welcome to my space. Stay, scroll, and feel something.
